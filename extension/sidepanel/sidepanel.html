Privacy & safety

  • Your profile values are never sent to the AI. The model only sees field names and returns references like profileKey: "email", which are filled in locally.
  • The AI can only request whitelisted actions (fill, select, check, click, navigate) — it never runs code on the page.
  • Passwords, OTPs, CAPTCHAs, card and ID numbers are never filled.
  • Payments and deletions always require your approval — even in Full Auto. Emails are sent only when your task says so.
